# Qush ovozlari — manbalar / Bird song credits

Saytdagi qush ovozlari (`webapp/birds/*.mp3`) Wikimedia Commons'dagi erkin
litsenziyali yozuvlardan olingan. Har biridan 11 soniyalik bo'lak kesilgan,
past g'uvillash va shovqin tozalangan, ovoz balandligi tenglashtirilgan,
boshi va oxiri sekin ochilib-yopiladi. O'zgartirilgan kliplar asl yozuv
litsenziyasi (CC BY-SA) ostida tarqatiladi.

The site's bird clips are cut from freely licensed recordings on Wikimedia
Commons: an 11-second excerpt each, high-pass filtered, de-noised, loudness
normalised, with a fade in and out. The adapted clips are shared under the
same licence as the original (CC BY-SA).
